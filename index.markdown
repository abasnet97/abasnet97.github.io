<section class="bio">
  <h2>About</h2>
    <p>I am an experimental high-energy physicist associated with the <a href="https://cms.cern/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Compact Muon Solenoid (CMS)</a> experiment at CERN. I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Texas A&amp;M University working with <a href="https://artsci.tamu.edu/physics-astronomy/contact/profiles/stephane-cooperstein.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dr. Stephane Cooperstein</a>. I completed my PhD at The Ohio State University in 2025 under the supervision of <a href="https://physics.osu.edu/people/winer.12" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dr. Brian Winer</a>.</p>

    <p>For my doctoral research, I searched for new physics in top-quark pairs produced in association with a photon using the model-agnostic framework of the Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT). As a postdoctoral researcher, I am working on measurements of non-resonant Higgs-boson pair production (HH) in the final state with four bottom quarks (4b). I am also involved in the Phase-II upgrade of the Outer Tracker Data AcQuisition (DAQ) system for the High-Luminosity Large Hadron Collider (HL-LHC). The HL-LHC is the successor of the LHC and is set to begin its operations from 2030.</p>

    <p>Beyond physics, I enjoy playing and watching tennis and working out. My favorite active tennis players are Carlos Alcaraz, Jannik Sinner, and Iga Swiatek. Roger Federer is an absolute idol to me. I am also an avid football fan, and I root for Manchester United (tough choice these days, I am aware).</p>
